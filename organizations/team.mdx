---
title: "Users"
description: "Invite people to your organization, change their roles, and remove access."
---

Go to **Settings → Users** to see everyone in the organization and manage their access.

You can invite anyone, whether or not they already use Surnex. They get an email; clicking **Accept invitation** signs them in and puts them straight into the organization. Until they click it they're **pending** and have no access — they can't see the organization or any of its projects' data until they accept.

## Invite a user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invite User">
    A panel opens from the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their full name and email address">
    Someone new gets a Surnex account under that name, which they can change later. Someone who already has an account keeps their own name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Pick a card:

    - **Admin** — full access to the organization, its projects and settings.
    - **Member** — works in every project: keywords, audits and reports.

    See [Roles and permissions](/organizations/roles) for exactly what each can do.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send invite">
    They appear in the users list straight away with the status **Invited**, and the email goes out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the email's link has expired by the time they open it, they sign in with their email as usual and land in the organization all the same. Inviting someone who is still pending sends the email again. Inviting someone already in the organization fails with *"That person is already a member."*

Every plan includes unlimited users, so there's no seat limit and no seat fee for an invitation. See [Plans](/billing/plans).

## The users list

The table has four columns:

- **Person** — initials, name and email. Your own row is tagged **(you)**.
- **Role** — owner, admin or member.
- **Status** — **Invited** for someone who hasn't accepted yet, **Active** for a member.
- **Joined** — the date they accepted, or *Invited* and the date the invitation was sent.

## Change someone's role

Click the pencil on their row, pick **Admin** or **Member** and save. Their access updates on their next request. Admins and owners can change roles; only an owner can change another owner's, so an owner's row has no pencil for an admin.

The pencil doesn't appear on your own row — you can't change your own role.

## Remove a user

Click the trash icon on their row and confirm. They lose access to the organization immediately, but their Surnex account and any other organizations they belong to are untouched.

You can't remove yourself here. An owner's row has no trash icon: another owner changes their role to admin or member first, then removes them. The last owner can't be demoted or removed — an organization with no owner can't be administered and isn't recoverable through the product.

Removing someone who is still **Invited** withdraws the invitation. The email's link still signs them in, because their Surnex account exists, but it no longer joins them to the organization.

## Who can do what

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Work inside projects | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create or delete a project | Yes | Yes | **No** |
| Add and remove members and admins | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change member and admin roles | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change or remove an owner | Yes | **No** | No |
| Rename the organization | Yes | Yes | No |
| View plan and usage | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Change plan or payment details | Yes | Yes | No |
| Delete the organization | Yes | **No** | No |

See [Roles and permissions](/organizations/roles) for the full breakdown.
