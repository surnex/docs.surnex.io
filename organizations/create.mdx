---
title: "Create an organization"
description: "Set up a new organization and become its owner."
---

You create your first organization during [onboarding](/onboarding/overview). You can create more at any time from the organization switcher in the sidebar.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the create form">
    From the organization switcher at the top of the sidebar, choose **Create organization**. The screen is headed **Add an organization**. If you have no organizations at all, Surnex sends you straight to it after sign-in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an organization name and website">
    For example, `Acme Corp` and `acme.com`. The name is shown in the switcher and on the [Organization settings](/organizations/settings) page, and you can change it later. The website is saved as the organization's (see [Organization settings](/organizations/settings)) and is what the sample project points at. The organization's timezone starts on UTC; you can change it in settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Organization">
    The organization is created, becomes your active one, and you land on its sample project's dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens on create

**A slug is generated.** Surnex slugifies the name — `Acme Corp` becomes `acme-corp`. If another organization already holds that slug, a number is appended: `acme-corp-2`, then `acme-corp-3`. The slug is fixed once generated and displayed read-only in settings.

**You become the owner.** The creator is the owner, and owner isn't given to anyone else — people you invite from the [Users](/organizations/team) page are admins or members.

**A sample project is created.** It's named after the organization, points at the website, and is filled with made-up data. It's read-only, has no schedules, and doesn't count against the plan. See [The sample project](/onboarding/overview#the-sample-project).

**Your real project comes next.** Create it with **Create Your Project** on the sample's banner, or **Add Project** on the Projects page — see [Create a project](/projects/add).

<Note>
  Creating a second organization does not copy anything from your first — no projects, no members, no keywords. Organizations are fully isolated.
</Note>

## When to create a second organization

Create one when the work needs its own team, its own billing, or hard isolation:

- An agency keeping each client's projects and invoices separate
- Separating a personal side project from company work
- A client who needs their own subscription

If you only need to separate *websites*, create more [projects](/projects/add) inside one organization instead — projects are the per-domain unit, and keeping them together means one plan and one team.
