title: "Organization settings"
description: "Rename your organization, change its company details and timezone, or delete it."
---

Go to **Settings → Organization**. The page has two sections: **Organization Information** and **Danger Zone**.

## Organization Information

| Field | Editable | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organization name** | Yes | The display name in the organization switcher and on invoices. |
| **Website** | Yes | Your company's site — the one you entered when you signed up. Type `acme.com`; `https://` is added for you. |
| **City**, **State or region**, **Country**, **Phone number** | Yes | Your company's details. |
| **Timezone** | Yes | Your organization's home timezone, **UTC** until you choose one — you're never asked at sign-up. Search by city, country, abbreviation (`EST`, `SAST`) or offset (`UTC+2`); common timezones are listed first, each with its local time and UTC offset. Each [schedule](/projects/settings#schedules) keeps its own timezone, so changing this never moves when anything runs. |

### Save changes

Edit any field and click **Save Changes** below the form; it stays disabled until something has changed. Only owners and admins can edit — members see the fields switched off.

Renaming updates the display name only. The organization's slug, generated once when it was created, doesn't change.

### Organization ID

The page doesn't show the organization's ID. It's the `{orgId}` segment in every dashboard URL — you'll need it when working directly with the [REST API](/api/introduction).

## Danger Zone

### Delete the organization

**Cancel the subscription first.** While the organization has a paid subscription that will renew, **Delete Organization** is disabled, with a notice beneath it and a **Go to Billing** button ([Billing](/billing/subscription)). Cancel it there; once it's cancelled — or if you're on a card-free trial — you can delete.

Click **Delete Organization**. A panel opens listing what goes; type the organization's name and click **Delete Organization**. This permanently removes the organization and **all of its projects, members, and collected data** — rankings, backlinks, audits, reports, and history. Whatever is left of a cancelled subscription ends at that moment. It cannot be undone. Only an owner can delete an organization.

**Its users are deleted with it** — their Surnex accounts and logins — unless they also belong to another organization, which they stay in.

After deletion, Surnex reloads your remaining organizations. If one is left you're taken to it; with several, you choose which to open. If it was your only organization, your account is deleted with it and you're signed out — the panel says so before you confirm.

<Warning>
  Deletion removes tracking history that can't be rebuilt. Rankings and backlink history are collected day by day — a new organization tracking the same domain starts from zero, with no back-fill. Export anything you need first.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Deleting an organization deletes your Surnex account only if it's your only organization. To remove your account while keeping an organization others use, see [Delete your account](/account/delete).
</Note>
