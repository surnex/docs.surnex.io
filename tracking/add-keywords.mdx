**Tracking** lists every keyword the project monitors, one card per keyword. This is where you manage what gets checked.

## Add keywords

Click **Add Keywords** to open the panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your keywords">
    The **Keywords** box is a multi-line textarea. Separate keywords with **commas** to add several at once:

    ```
    seo tools, rank tracker, backlink checker
    ```

    Each comma-separated value is trimmed and empty entries are dropped, so trailing commas and stray spaces are harmless. Line breaks are not separators — only commas are.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Search Engine">
    **Google**, **Bing**, **Yahoo**, **Baidu**, **Naver** or **Seznam**. Starts on what you chose for rank tracking when you created the project. Choose it first: the locations and languages offered are the engine's own.

    Baidu searches from China in Chinese, and Seznam in Czech, so choosing either moves Location and Language there. Naver shows the same results wherever it's searched from — it has no location or language to choose, and its keywords are checked in Korean.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Location">
    Starts on the project's rank tracking default. Any place the engine searches from — every country, and the regions, cities, districts and postal codes inside them, about 270,000 for Google. Type two letters to search, for example `austin`, `austin, texas` or `10001`. Countries are listed before the towns that share their name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Language">
    Starts on the project's rank tracking default. Lists the languages the engine searches in: about 130 for Google, about 50 for Bing, Simplified Chinese for Baidu, Czech for Seznam.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Add Keywords">
    All keywords in the box are added with the same location, language, and search engine. A confirmation names how many were added. A location or language the engine doesn't search from is refused with a message saying which, before anything is added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To track one keyword in several markets, add it once per market — a different location, language or search engine. Each is tracked as a separate keyword with its own history. Adding a keyword again in a market it's already tracked in skips it.

New keywords get a **first check of their own straight away** — only the keywords you added, not the whole project — and show a **Pending** badge until it completes, usually within about 20 minutes. Their search volume fills in with the next scheduled check, which looks up every missing volume at once. After that they follow the project's rank tracking schedule. Adding a keyword that's paused leaves it paused; resume it from its card.

## Reading a keyword card

Each card shows:

- The **keyword**, with either a **Pending** badge or `Checked 3 hours ago`, and its market — the engine and location, for example `Bing · Austin, Texas, United States`
- The **current position** in large type, `>100` when the last check didn't find it in the top 100, or `—` while it's **Pending** and hasn't been checked yet
- The **change** since the previous check, with an up, down, or flat arrow — or **New** when it came into the top 100 since then, and **Lost** when it fell out of it. `—` on its first check
- **Vol:** monthly search volume, when known. Volumes come from Google Ads, which covers 46 languages. A keyword tracked in a language it doesn't cover, such as Zulu, Afrikaans or `pt-BR` (use `pt` for Portuguese), is ranked as normal but has no volume
- The **ranking URL** — which of your pages actually ranks, which is often not the one you expected
- An **AI Overview** badge when Google returned an AI Overview for this keyword (Google keywords only). It reads **AI Cited** in green if your domain was cited in it, or **AI Overview** in purple if the overview appeared without you

Click a card to open its [history](/tracking/keyword-history).

## Active and All

The toggle in the header switches between **Active** (the default) and **All**. Paused keywords are hidden under **Active** and shown dimmed under **All**.

## Pause a keyword

Use the switch on the card. Pausing stops future rank checks but **keeps all collected history** — flip it back on and checking resumes, leaving a gap in the chart for the paused period.

Pausing doesn't make room under your keyword limit — a paused keyword still counts toward your plan's tracked-keyword limit. Only removing it frees the slot.

## Change a keyword's market

A tracked keyword can't be edited. Its history belongs to the market it was collected in, so a keyword moved to another location would chart two different search results as one line.

To track it somewhere else, [add it again](#add-keywords) with the new location, language or search engine. Keep the old one for its history, or remove it.

## Remove a keyword

Click the trash icon and confirm. Removal is permanent — the keyword and its **entire position history** are deleted and can't be recovered. If you might want the data later, [pause it](#pause-a-keyword) instead.

## Export

Open the **⋮** menu at the right end of the list's toolbar, next to the search box, and choose **Export as CSV** (opens in Excel and Google Sheets) or **Export as JSON**. You get your tracked keywords with their current positions.

The file holds every keyword matching the current search and the **Active** / **All** toggle — not just the page on screen — with the list's own columns, up to 10,000 rows. Exports read stored data, so they're free.

## AI Overview summary

When any tracked keyword has AI Overview data, three cards appear above the list:

| Card | Reads |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Overview Triggered** | `12 / 40` — keywords where an AI Overview appeared, out of all tracked |
| **Domain Cited in AI** | `5 / 12` — of those, how many cited your domain |
| **AI Citation Rate** | `42%` — the second figure as a percentage of the first |

The cards describe the **newest check** — what Google showed last time, not a count of every AI Overview ever seen. AI Overviews are Google's, so keywords tracked on other engines aren't counted.

## Plan limits

Tracked keywords are capped per plan, counted across the whole organization. At the limit, **Add Keywords** greys out with a padlock and the tooltip *"Upgrade to track more keywords"*, and an upgrade prompt appears on the page.

The cap counts every keyword you hold, paused or not; scheduled checks on them don't spend any monthly allowance. To make room, remove keywords you're not acting on — changing the check frequency doesn't free a slot. See [Plans](/billing/plans).

## Empty state

A project with no keywords shows **No tracked keywords** — "Add keywords to monitor their search engine rankings" — with an **Add Keywords** button. Projects created through the normal flow always start with at least one keyword, since [project creation](/projects/add) requires one.
