**Tracking** lists every keyword the project monitors, one card per keyword. This is where you manage what gets checked.

## Add keywords

Click **Add Keywords** to open the panel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your keywords">
    The **Keywords** box is a multi-line textarea. Separate keywords with **commas** to add several at once:

    ```
    seo tools, rank tracker, backlink checker
    ```

    Each comma-separated value is trimmed and empty entries are dropped, so trailing commas and stray spaces are harmless. Line breaks are not separators — only commas are.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Location">
    Defaults to **United States**. Searchable dropdown of roughly 185 locations.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Language">
    Defaults to **English**. Around 65 options.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Search Engine">
    **Google** (default), **Bing**, or **Yahoo**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Add Keywords">
    All keywords in the box are added with the same location, language, and search engine. A confirmation names how many were added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To track one keyword in several markets, add it once per market — a different location, language or search engine. Each is tracked as a separate keyword with its own history. Adding a keyword again in a market it's already tracked in skips it.

New keywords show a **Pending** badge until their first check completes.

## Reading a keyword card

Each card shows:

- The **keyword**, with either a **Pending** badge or `Checked 3 hours ago`
- The **current position** in large type, or `—` when it doesn't rank
- The **change** since the previous check, with an up, down, or flat arrow
- **Vol:** monthly search volume, when known
- The **ranking URL** — which of your pages actually ranks, which is often not the one you expected
- An **AI Overview** badge when Google returned an AI Overview for this keyword. It reads **AI Cited** in green if your domain was cited in it, or **AI Overview** in purple if the overview appeared without you

Click a card to open its [history](/tracking/keyword-history).

## Active and All

The toggle in the header switches between **Active** (the default) and **All**. Paused keywords are hidden under **Active** and shown dimmed under **All**.

## Pause a keyword

Use the switch on the card. Pausing stops future rank checks but **keeps all collected history** — flip it back on and checking resumes, leaving a gap in the chart for the paused period.

Pausing doesn't make room under your keyword limit — a paused keyword still counts toward your plan's tracked-keyword limit. Only removing it frees the slot.

## Change a keyword's market

A tracked keyword can't be edited. Its history belongs to the market it was collected in, so a keyword moved to another location would chart two different search results as one line.

To track it somewhere else, [add it again](#add-keywords) with the new location, language or search engine. Keep the old one for its history, or remove it.

## Remove a keyword

Click the trash icon and confirm. Removal is permanent — the keyword and its **entire position history** are deleted and can't be recovered. If you might want the data later, [pause it](#pause-a-keyword) instead.

## Export

**Export** appears in the header once you have at least one keyword, and downloads your tracked keywords with their current positions.

## AI Overview summary

When any tracked keyword has AI Overview data, three cards appear above the list:

| Card | Reads |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Overview Triggered** | `12 / 40` — keywords where an AI Overview appeared, out of all tracked |
| **Domain Cited in AI** | `5 / 12` — of those, how many cited your domain |
| **AI Citation Rate** | `42%` — the second figure as a percentage of the first |

The cards describe the **newest check** — what Google showed last time, not a count of every AI Overview ever seen.

## Plan limits

Tracked keywords are capped per plan, counted across the whole organization. At the limit, **Add Keywords** greys out with a padlock and the tooltip *"Upgrade to track more keywords"*, and an upgrade prompt appears on the page.

The cap counts every keyword you hold, paused or not; scheduled checks on them don't spend any monthly allowance. To make room, remove keywords you're not acting on — changing the check frequency doesn't free a slot. See [Plans](/billing/plans).

## Empty state

A project with no keywords shows **No tracked keywords** — "Add keywords to monitor their search engine rankings" — with an **Add Keywords** button. Projects created through the normal flow always start with at least one keyword, since [project creation](/projects/add) requires one.
