**GEO** — generative engine optimization — measures your presence in AI-generated answers at the level of a **prompt** — what a customer might ask an AI assistant — rather than a single query. Where [AI search](/ai/overview) tools are one-off lookups, GEO tracks prompts on a schedule so you can see the trend.

The page is headed **AI Visibility**.

## The six engines

Every prompt is put, live, to six AI engines on each snapshot:

| Engine | What's asked |
| --- | --- |
| **Google AI Overviews** | The AI summary at the top of Google's results |
| **Google AI Mode** | Google's conversational AI search |
| **ChatGPT** | OpenAI's assistant, searching the web |
| **Claude** | Anthropic's assistant, searching the web |
| **Gemini** | Google's assistant, searching the web |
| **Perplexity** | Perplexity's answer engine |

A prompt is measured in a **country** and a **language** that every one of the six engines takes: one of 215 countries, in one of about 95 languages (Zulu among them). AI answers are by country, and ChatGPT's search lists no cities. New prompts start on the AI visibility market you chose when you [created the project](/projects/add). Each engine is then asked what it accepts:

- **Google AI Overviews** and **Google AI Mode** — the prompt's country and language.
- **ChatGPT** and **Perplexity** — the prompt's country.
- **Claude** — the prompt's country if it's one of the 36 Claude's web search takes; otherwise Claude searches without one.
- **Gemini** — no country: its web search isn't localised.

An engine that's down is recorded as not answering, and the other five still count.

## Stat cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Prompts Tracked** | How many prompts you monitor |
| **Avg. Mentions** | How many of the six engines name your brand, on average per prompt |
| **Avg. Citation Rate** | The share of engines that cite your site, on average per prompt |

Open a prompt to see each engine's full answer, marked **Mentioned** or **Not mentioned**, and **Cited** or **Not cited**.

**Mentions** and **citations** aren't the same thing, and the distinction is the point of this page. A mention is the AI naming your brand in its answer; a citation is it linking your domain as a source. Being mentioned without being cited means you have brand awareness but the model isn't treating your site as the authority — that's a content problem. Being cited without being mentioned means your page is feeding answers that don't credit you.

## Tracked Prompts

The list under **Tracked Prompts** — "Prompts asked of all six AI engines every week" — is what gets checked on each snapshot.

### Add prompts

Click **Add Prompts** and enter prompts in the textarea, **one per line** — a keyword, or a question someone might ask an AI assistant. Commas don't separate prompts, because a question often contains them.

A prompt is tracked per location and language, so the same question in another market is a new prompt. One already tracked in that market is skipped — a paused one stays paused.

New prompts get their **first snapshot straight away**, from all six engines, and it's covered by your plan's AI prompts. After that they follow the AI visibility schedule — weekly by default.

Prompts are seeded automatically when a [project is created](/projects/add), so the list won't be empty on a new project.

### Pause a prompt

Toggle a prompt to pause it. Paused prompts stop being snapshotted but keep their history, and appear dimmed in the list. A paused prompt still counts against your plan's AI prompts — only removing it frees the slot.

### Remove a prompt

In **Remove prompt**, confirm *"Are you sure you want to remove '…'? This will stop tracking AI visibility for this prompt."* This deletes the prompt's history — pause instead if you might want it back.

## Choosing prompts

Prompts are the subjects you want to be the answer for, phrased the way someone would ask:

- **"best rank tracking tool for agencies"** works — a real question with room for a vendor in the answer.
- **"rank tracking"** is too broad. The answer will be a definition and won't cite vendors.
- **Your brand name** works only as a control, to confirm the system finds you at all.

Aim for questions where a good answer would naturally name a product like yours. If you can't imagine your company in the answer, tracking it will only ever produce zeros.

## Schedule

Set it in **Project Settings → Schedules**, under **AI visibility**: frequency (every day, every 3 days or once a week), hour and timezone, or pause it. See [Project settings](/projects/settings#schedules).

GEO defaults to **once a week at 00:00 UTC** — AI answers change slowly, and a week-on-week trend is the useful reading. Every day and every 3 days are there if you want them.

The **Timezone** is applied, and cadences are intervals from the last run rather than calendar rules. See [How data collection works](/projects/data-collection).

Each snapshot asks every active prompt of all six engines. Each prompt is one of your plan's **AI prompts**, and the scheduled snapshots are covered by holding it — they don't spend any monthly allowance, whatever the frequency. Asking about one prompt now, outside the schedule, spends one research lookup. See [Plans](/billing/plans).

## GEO vs. AI search

|  | GEO | [AI search](/ai/overview) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit | A prompt, tracked over time | A query, looked up now |
| Cadence | Scheduled, weekly by default | On demand |
| History | Yes — snapshots accumulate | Only past lookups |
| Question | "Is my share of voice growing?" | "Am I cited for this, right now?" |

Use GEO to measure progress and AI search to diagnose a specific answer.
