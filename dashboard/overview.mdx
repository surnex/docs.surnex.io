The **Dashboard** is where a project opens. It pulls the headline figures from each area of Surnex into one screen, each in the chart that suits it, so you can see what changed without visiting ten pages.

## Stat cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Avg. Position** | Mean position across your tracked keywords that rank, with how many are in the top 10 |
| **Domain Rank** | Your domain's authority score, 0–1,000, with its trend |
| **AI Overview Rate** | Share of tracked keywords where Google showed an AI Overview |
| **AI Citation Rate** | Share of AI engine answers for your [GEO](/geo/overview) topics that cite your site |

Each card shows its change since the previous reading. The four come from different systems — [rank tracking](/tracking/overview), [backlinks](/backlinks/overview), [AI search](/ai/overview) and GEO — and each updates on its own [collection schedule](/projects/data-collection), so they won't all be from the same moment.

**Avg. Position** excludes keywords that don't rank at all, so it can improve when a keyword falls out of the top 100 entirely. Read it alongside **Keyword Rankings**.

## Rankings

**Keyword Rankings** shows your tracked keywords as one bar, split by position band, with the count and share in each:

| Band | Positions |
| --- | --- |
| **Top 3** | 1–3 |
| **4–10** | 4–10 |
| **11–20** | 11–20 |
| **21–50** | 21–50 |
| **51–100** | 51–100 |
| **Not ranking** | Outside the top 100 |

The bands are exclusive, so they add up to your tracked keywords. A bar dominated by **Not ranking** and **51–100** means you're tracking keywords you're not competitive for yet — a useful target list, but **Avg. Position** only averages the few that rank.

**Top Movers**, beside it, lists the biggest position changes since the last check. It's empty on a new project: movement needs two checks to compare.

## AI search and GEO

- **Google AI Overviews** — one bar per check day for the last 30 days, split into keywords whose AI Overview cites you, keywords with an AI Overview that doesn't, and keywords with none
- **AI Engines** — for each of the six engines, how often its answers cite your site and how often they mention your brand, across your GEO topics
- **AI Visibility Trend** — the share of engine answers citing your site over the last 90 days

## Local

**Local Map** shows the [Local SEO](/local/overview) grid for your best-placed keyword: your position at each point around your listing, the share of points in the top 3, and your average position where you're found. **Open grid** goes to that keyword.

## Backlinks

- **Backlink Growth** — total backlinks and referring domains over the last 90 days, each on its own axis so both are readable
- **Link Profile** — the dofollow and nofollow split from the latest snapshot, with total backlinks and referring domains and their change

A profile that's nearly all dofollow can indicate bought links; one that's nearly all nofollow isn't passing authority.

## Site health

- **Audit Scores** — one bar per recent [site audit](/audits/overview), green from 80, amber from 50, red below
- **Site Health** — the latest audit's score as a gauge, with its errors, warnings and notices

Audits don't run on a schedule, so **Audit Scores** is the quickest way to notice your last crawl was a while ago.

## While data is being collected

A section with no data yet says why. If the job that fills it is queued or running — a rank check, an audit, an AI visibility snapshot, a backlink snapshot or a local check — the section shows it in progress, when it started, and how long that job usually takes. The first rankings arrive within minutes; a full site audit can take up to an hour.

When a job finishes, the dashboard refreshes that data in place. You don't need to reload or keep the page open.
