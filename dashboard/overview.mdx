The **Dashboard** is where a project opens. It pulls the headline number from each area of Surnex into one screen, so you can see what changed without visiting six pages.

## Stat cards

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Avg Position** | Mean position across your tracked keywords that rank |
| **Domain Rank** | Your domain's authority score |
| **AI Overview Rate** | Share of tracked keywords where Google returned an AI Overview |
| **GEO Citation Rate** | How often your brand is cited in AI answers for your topics |

The four are deliberately from different systems: [rank tracking](/tracking/overview), [backlinks](/backlinks/overview), [AI search](/ai/overview), and [GEO](/geo/overview). Each updates on its own [collection schedule](/projects/data-collection), so they won't all be from the same moment.

**Avg Position** excludes keywords that don't rank at all, so it can improve when a keyword falls out of the top 100 entirely. Read it alongside the distribution bar below rather than on its own.

## Ranking Distribution

A single stacked bar showing how your tracked keywords are spread across position bands:

| Segment | Positions |
| --- | --- |
| **Top 3** | 1–3 |
| **Top 10** | 4–10 |
| **Top 20** | 11–20 |
| **Top 50** | 21–50 |
| **Top 100** | 51–100 |
| **Not Ranking** | Outside the top 100 |

The bands are exclusive, so the segments sum to your total tracked keywords. Hover a segment for its label and count.

The shape matters more than any single number. A bar dominated by **Not Ranking** and **Top 100** means you're tracking keywords you're not competitive for yet — useful as a target list, but the **Avg Position** figure above will be misleading, since it only averages the minority that rank.

## Recent Audits

The latest [site audits](/audits/overview) with their scores and status. Empty state: **No audits yet** — "Run a site audit to see results".

Because audits don't run on a schedule, this panel is the quickest way to notice your last crawl was months ago.

## Top Movers

Keywords whose positions changed most recently, from the same data as [Position changes](/tracking/changes). Empty state: **No ranking changes** — "Position changes will appear here".

Empty on a new project: movement needs two rank checks to compare.

## Backlink Stats

Four figures from your [backlink profile](/backlinks/overview):

| Figure | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Referring Domains** | Distinct domains linking to you |
| **Total Backlinks** | Every individual link |
| **Dofollow / Nofollow** | The split between them |
| **Dofollow Ratio** | Dofollow links as a share of the total |

**Dofollow Ratio** is the one to watch over time. A natural profile has a healthy mix; a ratio that's very high or very low is worth investigating — near-total dofollow can indicate bought links, while near-total nofollow means your links aren't passing authority.

## A new project

Immediately after [creating a project](/projects/add), most of this is empty. Panels fill in as each background job finishes — the first rankings within minutes, a full site audit up to an hour. You don't need to keep the page open.
