**Settings → Billing** opens with a banner saying where your plan stands, then your usage against its limits, the four [plans](/billing/plans) side by side, and your [invoices](/billing/invoices).

## The trial

Every new organization starts on a 14-day trial of **Growth**, with no card. The banner says how many days are left, with **Choose a plan**.

Choosing a plan during the trial ends it: you're charged straight away, and the plan starts that day.

## Choosing or changing plan

Toggle between **Monthly** and **Yearly** to see both prices. Yearly is 10% off and is shown per month, with the yearly total underneath.

- **With no paid subscription** — on the trial, or after one ended — choosing a plan opens a Stripe checkout. You're returned to Surnex when it completes, and the plan is applied within a few seconds.
- **With a paid subscription**, the plan is changed in place, with no checkout. The change takes effect straight away and is prorated on your next invoice. Switching between monthly and yearly works the same way.

Choosing a plan also undoes a pending cancellation.

## Who can change it

**Admins and owners** can choose a plan or open the billing portal, and only from the dashboard — an API key can't.

Members can see the current plan and usage — they need that to know whether they can run an audit — but can't change what you pay. See [Roles and permissions](/organizations/roles).

## Billing portal

Once something has been bought, a **Billing portal** button appears at the top right of the page. It opens Stripe's portal, where you can:

- Update the card on file
- Download [invoices](/billing/invoices)
- Cancel the subscription

This is where cancellation happens — there's no cancel button inside Surnex. [Deleting the organization](/organizations/settings) also cancels its subscription, immediately.

## The plan banner

The banner at the top of **Settings → Billing** changes with your plan's state. Amber needs your attention soon; red means billing has stopped, or will.

| State | Banner | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free trial | Amber — **Free Growth trial — 14 days left**, with the end date | **Choose a plan** |
| Trial ended | Red — **Your free trial has ended**. Your data is kept; nothing new is checked | **Choose a plan** |
| No active plan | Red — **No active plan**, after a subscription ended | **Choose a plan** |
| Payment failed | Amber — **Your last payment failed**, while Stripe retries the card | **Update payment**, which opens the billing portal |
| Cancelled, still active | Red — **Growth is cancelled**, active until the end of the period | **Keep my plan**, which undoes the cancellation |
| Paid plan in its trial | Blue — the first charge date | — |
| Active | The plan, its price, and the renewal date | — |

Only admins and owners see the buttons. **Keep my plan** keeps the billing period you already pay, monthly or yearly.

## Upgrading

Takes effect immediately. New limits apply at once, so a blocked **Add Keywords** button unblocks as soon as you're back.

## Downgrading

Check your current usage first. A move to a plan the organization already holds more of than it allows — say 300 tracked keywords onto Starter's 200 — is refused, listing what's over. Remove the extra first, then change plan.

Only held allowances block a downgrade: tracked keywords, AI prompts and local locations. What you've already spent this month isn't undone; the smaller monthly allowance applies to what's left of the month.

<Warning>
  Removing keywords, topics or locations is permanent for their history. Export or [report](/reports/share) anything you need before downgrading.
</Warning>

## When a trial or subscription ends

The organization keeps reading everything already collected, but nothing billable runs — no scheduled checks, no new projects, no lookups, no audits. Choose a plan to start collecting again. A run skipped while there was no plan isn't made up afterwards.

A subscription whose renewal payment failed keeps its access while Stripe retries the payment. Update the card in the billing portal to keep the plan.

## Per organization

Subscriptions belong to an organization. If you own two, you pay for two — and upgrading one changes nothing about the other. Switch organizations in the sidebar switcher before checking billing so you're looking at the right one.

## Through the API

| Endpoint | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /v1/plans` | The four plans, with `price` in US cents (`year` is the whole year's charge), `limits` and `features` |
| `GET /v1/organizations/{orgId}/billing/subscription` | `plan_id`, `status`, `billing_interval`, `cancel_at_period_end`, `current_period_start`, `current_period_end`, `is_card_free_trial` and `active_plan` — which is `null` when nothing is live. `status` is `trialing`, `active`, `past_due`, `canceled`, `unpaid`, or `trial_expired` for a card-free trial that has ended (set within the hour after it does) |
| `POST /v1/organizations/{orgId}/billing/checkout` | Body `{ plan, interval, success_url, cancel_url }`, where `plan` is `starter`, `growth`, `agency` or `scale` and `interval` is `month` (default) or `year`. Answers `{ url, changed }`: a checkout `url` to send the buyer to, or `url: null` and `changed: true` when an existing subscription was changed in place |
| `POST /v1/organizations/{orgId}/billing/portal` | Body `{ return_url }`. Answers `{ url }` for the billing portal |

A checkout to a plan the organization holds more than answers `CONFLICT` (409) with the overages in `details`. Checkout and portal need the admin or owner role and refuse API keys.

`success_url`, `cancel_url` and `return_url` must be addresses on the Surnex app, `app.surnex.io`. Anything else answers `BAD_REQUEST` (400), *"Return addresses must be on the Surnex app."*, so a billing page can never send someone on to a site that isn't Surnex.
