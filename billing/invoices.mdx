Your invoices are listed in Surnex, and payment details are kept by the payment provider, Stripe.

## Invoices in Surnex

**Settings → Billing** lists every invoice for the organization under **Invoices**, newest first, once something has been bought. Each shows:

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | When the invoice was issued |
| **Invoice** | Its number — the one a receipt or your accountant quotes |
| **Description** | What it was for, and the billing period |
| **Amount** | What was paid, or for an unpaid invoice, what's owed |
| **Status** | **Paid**, **Due** (issued and not yet paid), **Void**, or **Unpaid** |

Every invoice has **View**, which opens it on Stripe, and **PDF** to download it. A due invoice's **View** reads **Pay**. Twelve are shown at a time; **Show Older Invoices** loads more.

The list is read from Stripe each time you open the page, so a payment that's just gone through, a retried card or a refund shows as Stripe has it now.

## Billing portal

**Billing Portal**, at the top right of **Settings → Billing**, opens Stripe's portal. It appears once something has been bought. There you can:

- Update the card on file
- Change your billing email
- Update billing address and tax details
- Change or cancel the subscription

## Who can access it

**Admins and owners** see the invoices and can open the billing portal. Members can see the current plan and usage in Surnex but not invoices or payment details. See [Roles and permissions](/organizations/roles).

If your finance team needs invoices, either make the person handling them an admin, or set the billing email to a shared finance address in the portal so invoices arrive there automatically.

## Billing email

Invoices go to the billing email held by the provider, which is set in the portal and isn't necessarily your Surnex login address. Point it at a finance inbox if that's who needs the receipts.

## One subscription per organization

Each [organization](/organizations/overview) bills separately, with its own invoices. Owning two means two subscriptions and two invoice histories — switch organizations in the sidebar first, or you'll be looking at the other one's invoices.

For agencies billing clients separately, that separation is the intended pattern: one organization per client keeps invoices, usage, and access cleanly divided.

## Cancelling

Cancellation is done in the portal — there's no cancel action inside Surnex. Cancelling stops future billing; it doesn't delete your data. To remove everything, [delete the organization](/organizations/settings) as well, which is permanent.
