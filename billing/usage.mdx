**Settings → Billing** has a **Usage** section showing what the organization holds and has used against its plan's limits.

## What's tracked

| Metric | Kind | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI prompts** | Held | [GEO](/geo/overview) (generative engine optimization) prompts across every project |
| **Tracked keywords** | Held | Every tracked keyword, active and paused |
| **Local locations** | Held | [Local SEO](/local/locations) locations — each a Google Business listing and its grid |
| **Research lookups this month** | Monthly | Every on-demand keyword, SERP, trends, domain and backlink lookup, keyword research jobs, and GEO questions asked outside the schedule — one per prompt |
| **AI brand audits this month** | Monthly | One per AI search run — AI Mode, ChatGPT visibility, LLM benchmark, AI keyword trends, citation gap |
| **Site audit pages this month** | Monthly | Pages crawled by site audits, plus one per Core Web Vitals check |

Each is shown against its plan limit. The numbers per plan are on [Plans](/billing/plans).

<Note>
  **A cached answer is free.** When Surnex already has a recent answer to the exact question — whoever first asked it — it's served without spending a research lookup.

  **A local location is a listing, not a keyword.** Up to 10 keywords searched around the same listing are one location, however big its grid.
</Note>

## Two kinds of limit

**Held limits** — AI prompts, tracked keywords, local locations — count what currently exists across the whole organization. Every row counts, paused or not. Pausing a keyword doesn't free its slot; removing it does, immediately.

**Monthly limits** — research lookups, AI brand audits, site audit pages — count what's been spent this calendar month. They reset on the **1st of each month, UTC**, and you can't free them by deleting anything. There are no daily allowances.

Knowing which kind you've hit tells you what to do. A held limit is fixed by removing something; a monthly limit is fixed by waiting for the 1st or upgrading.

## Site audits spend pages

A site audit costs the pages it crawls, not a flat fee per audit:

- When a crawl starts, its page limit is lowered to what's left of this month's allowance. The audit is refused only when nothing is left.
- That page limit is set aside while the crawl runs, so two audits started together can't both spend the same remainder.
- When the crawl finishes, pages it didn't crawl are given back.

With [Render JavaScript](/audits/run#render-javascript) on, each page crawled costs **4 pages** of the allowance, so the page limit is lowered to a quarter of what's left.

Each [Core Web Vitals check](/web-vitals/overview) uses one site audit page.

## Scheduled collection is included

Scheduled checks don't spend the monthly allowances:

- A daily [rank check](/tracking/overview) on 400 keywords is covered by holding 400 tracked keywords
- Scheduled [GEO](/geo/overview) snapshots are covered by your AI prompts; asking about a prompt now, or a snapshot taken on request through the API, spends a research lookup per prompt
- [Local SEO](/local/overview) checks are covered by your local locations
- [Backlink snapshots](/backlinks/overview) run on their cadence at no cost to your research lookups

Running out of research lookups never stops scheduled collection.

If the organization holds more than its plan allows — after a downgrade, for example — scheduled checks cover the oldest rows up to the limit, counted across the whole organization. The newest ones aren't checked until you remove enough to fit or upgrade.

## A new project's first run spends

[Creating a project](/projects/add#what-happens-next) starts its first collection straight away, and the on-demand parts of it spend like the buttons would:

- **About five research lookups** — domain overview, tech stack, the backlink profile, keyword research on the domain, and bulk research on your keywords
- **One AI brand audit** — an LLM benchmark on your first keyword
- **Site audit pages** — a crawl of up to 100 pages, plus one for a web vitals check

The bulk research and the benchmark only run if you added keywords. The first rank check, backlink snapshot and GEO snapshot are covered by what you hold, like any scheduled run. Local SEO has no first run — its first check starts when you add keywords at a location, and is covered by your local locations. The [sample project](/onboarding/overview#the-sample-project) spends nothing.

## At the limit

| Limit reached | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Tracked keywords | **Add Keywords** shows a padlock and *"Upgrade to track more keywords"* |
| AI prompts, local locations | Adding more is refused |
| Research lookups, AI brand audits | New lookups and runs are refused until the 1st |
| Site audit pages | New audits start smaller, and are refused once nothing is left |

An upgrade prompt appears while you're at a limit. The API answers `USAGE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (402) — see [Errors](/api/concepts/errors).

## No plan

When a trial or subscription ends, every limit reads zero. Everything already collected stays readable, but nothing new runs — scheduled checks stop, and lookups, audits and new projects are refused with `PLAN_REQUIRED` (402). Choose a plan on the [Billing](/billing/subscription) page to start again.

## Getting more from a plan

1. **Remove what you're not acting on.** A paused keyword still holds its slot; removing it frees the slot.
2. **Size audits to the site.** A crawl only spends the pages it crawls, so a page limit close to the site's real size wastes nothing.
3. **Reuse recent answers.** Repeating a lookup Surnex has a cached answer for is free.

## Who can see it

Every member can view usage, whatever their role — you need it to know whether an audit will run. Only admins and owners can change the plan. See [Roles and permissions](/organizations/roles).

## Through the API and MCP

`GET /v1/organizations/{orgId}/billing/usage` returns the same summary: the active `plan` (`null` when there's none), `resets_at` for the next monthly reset, and one entry per metric in `metrics`, each with `metric`, `kind` (`held` or `monthly`), `used`, `limit` and `remaining`. `features` says whether the plan includes `white_label` and `custom_report_domain`.

The metric names are `ai_prompts`, `keywords_tracked`, `local_locations`, `research_lookups`, `ai_brand_audits` and `site_audit_pages`. The MCP tool `get_usage_summary` returns the same summary.
