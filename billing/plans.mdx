Plans are per [organization](/organizations/overview). Belonging to two organizations means two subscriptions, and limits are counted separately for each.

There is no free plan. Every new organization starts on a **14-day trial of Growth**, with no card required — see [Trial](#trial) below.

## What each limit counts

The first three are **held** — a count of what the organization has right now, across all its projects. The monthly ones are what it **spends**, and reset on the 1st of each calendar month (UTC).

- **AI prompts** — the [GEO](/geo/overview) (generative engine optimization) topics you track, each asked of the AI assistants the GEO page tracks.
- **Tracked keywords** — every keyword under [rank tracking](/tracking/overview), paused or not.
- **Local locations** — the [locations](/local/locations) Local SEO searches from: your Google Business listing and a grid of Maps searches around it. Each takes up to 10 keywords, and keywords don't add a location.
- **Research lookups** — every on-demand question: keyword research, SERP results, Google Trends, domain analysis, backlink profile refreshes and gap analyses, live backlink and domain lookups, the content tools, and keyword research jobs. An answer Surnex already has cached is free.
- **AI brand audits** — each [AI search](/ai/overview) run counts as one, whichever it is: Google AI Mode, ChatGPT visibility, the LLM benchmark, AI keyword trends, or a citation gap.
- **Site audit pages** — pages crawled, not audits run. Each [Core Web Vitals check](/web-vitals/overview) uses one page.

See [Usage and limits](/billing/usage) for how each is checked.

## Scheduled collection is included

The rank checks, GEO snapshots, local checks and backlink snapshots that run on a [schedule](/projects/data-collection) don't spend the monthly allowances. They're what the held allowances pay for: 400 tracked keywords means 400 keywords checked on their schedule, however often that is.

A new project's first run is the exception worth knowing: it starts on creation and spends about five research lookups, one AI brand audit, and a site audit of up to 100 pages. On Starter, that's half the month's AI brand audits. See [Usage and limits](/billing/usage).

## Trial

A new organization gets 14 days of **Growth**, with no card. Choosing a plan during the trial ends it — you're charged straight away.

## When a trial or subscription ends

The organization keeps reading everything already collected. Nothing billable runs: no scheduled checks, no new projects, no lookups, no audits. The API answers these with `PLAN_REQUIRED` (402). Choose a plan on the [Billing](/billing/subscription) page to start collecting again.

A trial stops at the minute it ends. The organization switcher and the Billing page then say **Trial ended**.

A subscription whose renewal payment failed keeps its access while the payment is retried.

## Hitting a limit

Limits are enforced before the work happens rather than by billing you for the overage:

- **At the keyword limit**, **Add Keywords** shows a padlock and *"Upgrade to track more keywords"*.
- **At a monthly limit**, the action is refused until the allowance resets on the 1st, or until you upgrade.
- **For a site audit**, the crawl's page limit is lowered to what's left this month. It's refused only when nothing is left.

The API answers these with `USAGE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` (402). See [Errors](/api/concepts/errors).

## Getting more from a plan

- **Remove keywords you're not acting on.** Pausing keeps history and stops checks, but a paused keyword still counts against the tracked-keyword limit — only removing it frees the slot.
- **Size an audit's page limit to the site.** Audits cost pages crawled, so a crawl sized to the site spends only what it needs.
- **Reuse recent answers.** A question Surnex already has a cached answer for costs nothing, so repeating a recent lookup is free.
