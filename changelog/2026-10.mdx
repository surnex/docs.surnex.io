<Update label="1 October 2026" description="Exports and refreshes">

### Export any table

Every data table now has a **⋮** menu — three vertical dots in a bordered button at the right end of its toolbar, next to the search box, or in the card's title row when the table has no search box. It offers **Export as CSV**, which opens in Excel and Google Sheets, and **Export as JSON**.

- **Every matching row.** An export holds every row matching the table's current search, not just the page on screen, with the table's own columns, up to 10,000 rows.
- **Free.** Exports read stored data and spend no allowance.
- **The header Export buttons are gone** from Rank Tracking, Backlinks and the audit detail page — each of their tables has its own menu instead. Reports keep their PDF and CSV export on [Sharing & Export](/reports/share), and the API's CSV export endpoints are unchanged.

### Paid refreshes ask first

A refresh you start yourself spends your monthly allowance, so it now opens a confirm saying what it fetches again, what it costs and how many you have left this month. That covers **Refresh** on Backlinks, Domain Overview and Tech Stack, and running a past search again from a history list (1 research lookup each); **Check Now** on an AI prompt (1 research lookup); re-running an AI search — AI Mode, ChatGPT, LLM benchmark, citation gap, AI keyword trends (1 AI brand audit); and re-checking a Web Vitals URL (1 site audit page). See [Usage and limits](/billing/usage#refreshes-ask-first).

### One button on Backlinks

The Backlinks header has one button, **Refresh**, for the first analysis and every one after it, in place of **Run Analysis** and **Refresh**. It fetches the backlinks, referring domains, anchors and new and lost lists again. The scheduled backlink snapshot updates only the totals and the history chart, not those lists. See [Backlinks](/backlinks/overview#running-an-analysis).

</Update>

<Update label="1 October 2026" description="A market for each service">

### Each part of Surnex has its own market

Rank tracking, keyword research and AI visibility use different data sources, and each takes its own places and languages. A project now has a market for each, and every picker offers only what its source takes. Nothing is switched to another market behind your back.

- **Creating a project.** Four steps now; Local SEO locations are added from the Local SEO page once the project exists. **Competitors** says **Skip** until you add one. *Where to Measure* has a section each for rank tracking, keyword research and AI visibility, closed, each showing its market on one line. Research and AI visibility start on your rank tracking choice; when a source doesn't take it (South Africa in Zulu has no research), the nearest market it does is preselected, marked **Nearest supported**, and the section says why. See [Add a project](/projects/add).
- **No markets in Project Settings.** Each keyword and prompt has its own, chosen when it's added; Keyword Research has a country and language in each tab's form; Trends and the AI search pages open theirs in a panel from the button beside the page title, like Add Keywords. Each starts on the project's. See [Project Settings](/projects/settings).
- **AI visibility** is measured by country, in a language all six engines answer in, so every prompt is answered by all six. New prompts and AI searches start on the AI visibility market chosen when the project was created. See [AI visibility](/geo/overview).
- **Topics are now prompts.** What AI visibility tracks is called a prompt, matching the plans' *AI prompts*. **Add Prompts** takes one prompt per line — commas no longer separate them, since a question often contains one. See [AI visibility](/geo/overview#add-prompts).
- **Search volume.** Languages Google Ads has no volume in are marked *Ranked, but no search volume* when you add keywords.
- **Local SEO** locations have their own Google Maps language.
- **Google Trends** says clearly when the language chosen isn't one Trends covers (Norwegian `nb`), rather than failing.
- **API and MCP.** Projects return `tracking_location_code`, `tracking_language_code`, `ai_location_code` and `ai_language_code` alongside the research market (`location_code`, `language_code`), and take them on create and update — on create as `research_*`, `ai_*` and `local_*`. A market left out is the tracking one when its service takes it, and refused with the fields to send when it doesn't. New: `GET /v1/reference/ai-visibility/languages` and `GET /v1/reference/ai-visibility/countries`; `list_search_languages` takes `for: tracking | ai_visibility | research`.

### Removed

- **Content tools.** The **Content Tools** page (Grammar Check and Paraphrase), **Generate Meta Tags** on a site audit and **Content Brief** on Keyword Research are gone, with the API routes `/v1/content/meta-tags`, `/v1/content/brief`, `/v1/content/grammar` and `/v1/content/paraphrase` and the MCP tools `generate_meta_tags`, `generate_content_brief`, `check_grammar` and `paraphrase_text`. DataForSEO retired the Content Generation API they ran on. The MCP server now has 95 tools.

</Update>
