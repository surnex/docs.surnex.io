Onboarding is one screen, shown straight after your first sign-in. It creates your organization and a sample project, and opens the sample's dashboard. Your real project is created afterwards, from inside the app.

## The form

- **Your name** — asked only if your profile doesn't have one yet.
- **Organization name** — usually your company, for example `Acme Inc.`
- **Website** — your site, for example `acme.com`. You can paste a full URL; everything after the host is dropped.

Click **Continue to Your Dashboard**.

If something fails partway, the screen offers a retry without asking for your details again, and a retry doesn't create a second organization.

## What gets created

**Your organization.** It holds your projects, team, and billing.

- Surnex generates a URL-safe **slug** from the name — `Acme Corp` becomes `acme-corp`. If another organization already holds that slug, a number is added (`acme-corp-2`). The slug is generated once and shown read-only in [Organization settings](/organizations/settings).
- You are added as the **owner**. Owner isn't given to anyone else; people you invite are admins or members.
- The organization starts on a 14-day trial of the **Growth** plan, with no card required. See [Plans](/billing/plans).

**A sample project**, named after your organization and pointed at your website. See below.

## The sample project

The sample shows every stored-data page already filled in, so your first screen shows what Surnex does rather than a row of empty states. **Every figure in it is made up** — nothing was collected from your site. A banner at the top of each of its pages says so, and it carries a **Sample** badge on the Projects page.

It includes a [Local SEO](/local/overview) location with its Maps grids, and a **Weekly Overview** [report](/reports/list) you can open — the report isn't scheduled, so nothing is emailed.

- **It's read-only.** You can't add keywords, run an audit, or change its settings. Project settings shows its name, market and schedules with every control switched off.
- **It costs nothing.** It has no schedules, runs no jobs, and doesn't count against any allowance or your project count.
- **It stays current.** Its dates move forward daily, so date ranges like "last 7 days" always have data.
- **It can be removed, not rebuilt.** **Remove sample** on the banner deletes it. An organization only ever gets one.

The sample uses your own domain, and that doesn't block you: your first real project can track the same domain.

## Create your real project

Click **Create your project** on the sample's banner, or **Add Project** on the Projects page. It's the same [Create project](/projects/add) form either way, and it needs the admin or owner role.

Creating it starts collection straight away — rankings within minutes, everything else as each job finishes. See [How data collection works](/projects/data-collection) for the schedule each feature follows afterwards.

## Adding another organization

**Create organization** in the organization switcher opens the same screen, headed **Add an organization**. It doesn't ask for your name again, and the new organization gets its own sample project.
