The **Inbox** is where Surnex tells you what happened. Open it from your avatar menu in the sidebar, or go to `app.surnex.io/<organization>/inbox`.

The header says how many notifications are unread, or **You're all caught up** when none are.

## Your inbox is yours

Every member of an organization has their own inbox. Reading, selecting or deleting a notification changes it for you and nobody else.

The Inbox is always on: every notification lands here, and it can't be switched off. To get fewer emails, switch **Email** off under **Your notifications** in [Channels](/notifications/preferences) — it only affects you.

## Search and filter

The header holds everything the list is controlled from:

- **Search** — matches a notification's title or message
- **Filter** — the filter button opens two choices, and shows how many are active
  - **Show** — **Everything**, or **Unread only**
  - **Category** — **Rankings**, **Backlinks**, **Site audits**, **AI visibility**, **Local SEO**, **Web vitals** or **Reports**
- **Settings** — opens [Channels](/notifications/preferences)

**Clear** in the filter resets both choices.

## Select, mark read, delete

Hover a notification and its icon becomes a checkbox. Tick one, and every notification on the page shows its checkbox, so you can keep picking.

A bar appears above the list while anything is ticked:

- **Select All** — tick every notification on the page
- **Mark as Read**
- **Delete** — confirmed first: *"They'll be removed from your inbox. Nobody else's is touched. This can't be undone."*
- **×** — clear the selection

Changing the search, a filter or the page clears the selection. Opening a notification marks it read and shows its details.

## What each notification shows

| Category | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Rankings** | Each keyword that moved, from its old position to its new one, and by how much. **Lost** when it stopped ranking, **New** when it started |
| **Backlinks** | The metric that changed — backlinks, referring domains or domain rank — before and after, and the percentage. A referring-domains spike is flagged in amber: it can be spam links pointed at the site |
| **Site audits** | The audit's score, and its errors, warnings and notices |
| **AI visibility** | How many prompts' answers cite your site, how many answers mention your brand, and how many engines were asked |
| **Local SEO** | Keywords checked, how many grid points found you, and the share in the top 3 |
| **Web vitals** | The performance score, on which device, and which page |
| **Reports** | That a scheduled report is ready, with **Open report** |

Notifications are grouped by **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week** and **Older**, newest first.

## Empty states

| State | Message |
| --- | --- |
| **Unread only**, and nothing unread | **You're all caught up** |
| Nothing at all | **Your inbox is empty** |
| A search or category that matches nothing | **No matching notifications** — clear it to see the rest |

## Inbox vs. the ranking alert feed

They overlap, and it's worth knowing which to use:

|  | Inbox | [Position changes](/tracking/changes) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Everything, across your projects | Ranking alerts for one project |
| Grouping | By day, filtered by category | By date, with type filters |
| Extra views | — | Biggest Movers over a chosen date range |

Use the Inbox to check whether anything has happened you should know about. Use [Position changes](/tracking/changes) when you're investigating ranking movement, because it lets you compare date ranges.

Being notified doesn't mark the matching [ranking alerts](/tracking/changes) read.

## Not seeing something

If a notification you expected isn't here, check two things in order:

1. **Did the underlying job run?** A notification only arrives when the job finishes — see [How data collection works](/projects/data-collection).
2. **Did the event meet its threshold?** Ranking alerts need a 5+ position move or a band crossing. See [Ranking alerts](/tracking/alerts).

Notifications sent before you joined an organization aren't in your inbox.
