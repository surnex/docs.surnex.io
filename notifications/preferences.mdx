**Settings → Channels** has two sections: the channels that are **yours**, and the ones that are **the team's**.

## Your notifications

These are yours alone. Switching one off changes it for you in this organization, and nobody else on the team is affected. Any member can change their own.

| Channel | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | On | Notifications in your [Inbox](/notifications/view) in Surnex |
| **Email** | On | Notifications emailed to your sign-in address, which the page shows |

Switch **Email** off to stop Surnex's emails reaching you. Your teammates keep getting theirs.

Each organization you're in has its own switches, so you can keep email on for one and off for another.

### Scheduled reports

[Automated reports](/reports/automation) are always emailed to the recipients the automation names, whatever anyone's Email switch says. The switch covers Surnex's own notifications, not a delivery somebody set up on purpose.

## Team channels

Slack and Discord belong to the **organization**. Each posts once per notification, into one channel, for everyone who reads it. Only an **owner** or **admin** can connect, change or disconnect them.

| Channel | Default | Setup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slack** | Off | An incoming webhook URL (`https://hooks.slack.com/services/…`) |
| **Discord** | Off | An incoming webhook URL (`https://discord.com/api/webhooks/…`) |

Click **Connect** on the channel and paste its own incoming webhook. The URL must use HTTPS and be Slack's or Discord's webhook address exactly; anything else — another host, a proxy, a port — is refused.

- **Configure** — change the webhook, or pause the channel without removing it. A paused channel shows **Paused**
- **Disconnect** — turns the channel off and deletes the stored webhook, so reconnecting means pasting a new one

A webhook URL is the credential that posts into your channel, so members see only its host (`https://hooks.slack.com/…`). Owners and admins see the full URL.

## Events

Every event is on by default for every channel that's on. An owner or admin can turn an event off for a channel, for the whole organization, with the API's `PUT /v1/organizations/{orgId}/notifications/rules`.

### Ranking

| Event | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| **Rank Improved** (`rank_improved`) | A keyword improved by 5+ positions |
| **Rank Dropped** (`rank_dropped`) | A keyword dropped by 5+ positions |
| **Entered Top 3** (`entered_top3`) | A keyword entered positions 1–3 |
| **Entered Top 10** (`entered_top10`) | A keyword entered positions 1–10 |
| **Lost Top 10** (`lost_top10`) | A keyword fell out of the top 10 |
| **Lost Top 100** (`lost_top100`) | A keyword stopped ranking |

These match the [ranking alerts](/tracking/alerts) exactly — the thresholds are the same. A rank check sends one notification per alert type it raised, so turning off **Rank Improved** silences only improvements. The alerts themselves are always generated and always appear in the feed.

### Audits

**Audit Complete** — a site audit finished processing.

### Backlinks

| Event | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| **Backlinks Dropped** (`lost_backlinks`) | Total backlinks fell by 20% or more |
| **Referring Domains Dropped** (`referring_domains_dropped`) | Referring domains fell by 10% or more |
| **Referring Domains Spike** (`referring_domains_spike`) | Referring domains rose by 50% or more — flagged as potential spam |
| **Domain Rank Dropped** (`domain_rank_dropped`) | Domain rank fell by 50+ points |

Each change is its own notification, so each event can be turned off without losing the others.

**Referring Domains Spike** is the one worth keeping even if you turn off the rest. A sudden 50% jump in referring domains is more often a negative-SEO or spam-link event than a marketing success, and it's the kind of thing you want to hear about immediately.

### AI Visibility, Local SEO, Web Vitals

- **Snapshot Complete** — an AI visibility snapshot finished
- **Rankings Check Complete** — a [local SEO](/local/overview) check finished, with how many keywords were checked, how many grid points found you, and the share in the top 3
- **Vitals Check Complete** — a Core Web Vitals audit finished

### Reports

**Scheduled Report Ready** — an [automated report](/reports/automation) has been generated.

## Choosing what to keep

The completion events — audit, snapshot, local, vitals — fire every time a job finishes. On a project checking daily, that's a notification a day per feature. If your email gets noisy, switch **Email** off for yourself and read them in the [Inbox](/notifications/view) instead.

The events worth pushing to Slack are the ones the team would act on: **Lost Top 10**, **Lost Top 100**, **Referring Domains Spike** and **Domain Rank Dropped**.

## Permissions

| Change | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Your own **Inbox** and **Email** switches | Any member, for themselves |
| Connecting, pausing or disconnecting Slack and Discord | Owners and admins |
| Event rules for the organization | Owners and admins |

Members see the team channels with webhook URLs masked. See [Roles and permissions](/organizations/roles).
