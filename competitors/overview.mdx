Competitors in Surnex are a **per-project list of domains**, maintained on the [Competitors](/competitors/add) page.

## How competitor data is collected

Competitors aren't crawled or monitored as projects in their own right. Their data is a by-product of the collection Surnex already does for you:

- **Positions** are read from the same search results page as yours during a [rank check](/tracking/overview). Same keyword, same location, same language, same moment — so the comparison is genuinely like-for-like.
- **Backlink and AI data** is looked up against your project's [collection schedule](/projects/data-collection). Backlink totals are read for your five oldest competitors, at most once a week.

Two consequences follow. Comparisons are always same-day rather than stitched together from separate lookups. And a competitor only has data for keywords **you** track — adding a competitor doesn't reveal what else they rank for.

To see a competitor's full keyword profile, use [Domain Keywords](/keywords/research) on the Keywords page, which works on any domain.

## Competitors vs. domain analysis

Two different tools that people reach for interchangeably:

|  | Competitors list | [Domain overview](/domains/overview) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope | Your tracked keywords | The whole domain |
| Ongoing | Yes, collected on schedule | One lookup at a time |
| Answers | "Who beats me where I compete?" | "How strong is this site overall?" |

Use domain analysis to decide **whether** a domain is worth adding, then add it to the competitor list to track it over time.

## Limits

- Competitors are per project. A domain you compete with across several projects has to be added to each.
- Up to **10** can be seeded at [project creation](/projects/add). Adding them afterwards on the Competitors page has no stated cap, but every additional domain widens the comparison table.
- There's no competitor-level alerting. Nothing tells you when a competitor overtakes you; check the [comparison table](/tracking/competitors) or your own [ranking alerts](/tracking/alerts) for the drop.
