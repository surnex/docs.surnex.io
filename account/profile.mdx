**Settings → Profile** covers your personal account — the one that spans every organization you belong to, unlike organization settings which apply to one.

## What's here

**Avatar** — your profile picture, or your initials if you don't have one. Shown next to your name on the [Users](/organizations/team) page.

**Full name** — the only editable field. Click it to switch into edit mode, type the new name, then **Save** or **Cancel**. A confirmation reads *"Profile updated"*.

Your name is what others see in the users list. If it's blank, they see your email address instead — worth setting if you share an organization with anyone.

**Email** — read-only. This is your sign-in address and the one an admin must use to [add you to an organization](/organizations/team). It can't be changed here.

**Member since** — when your account was created.

## Personal vs. organization

Easy to conflate, so:

| Setting | Scope | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your name and avatar | You, everywhere | This page |
| Your notifications | You, per organization | [Notification preferences](/notifications/preferences) |
| Organization name | The organization | [Organization settings](/organizations/settings) |
| Your role | Per organization | Set by an admin on [Users](/organizations/team) |

Changing your name updates it in every organization you belong to at once.
