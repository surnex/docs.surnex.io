---

Surnex doesn't use passwords. You sign in with Google, or with a one-time link emailed to you, so there's nothing to change, reset or forget. See [Sign in](/authentication).

## Can't get in?

Go to the [sign-in page](https://app.surnex.io/signin), enter your email address and click **Email Me a Sign-In Link**. You need access to the mailbox for that address — if you've lost it, or signed up with Google, sign in the same way you did before.

## Keeping your account safe

Your account reaches every organization you belong to — including client data if you work in an agency — so its safety is your mailbox's (or your Google account's). Use two-step verification there.

## Sessions

Your session persists across tabs and browser restarts. Sign out from the user menu in the sidebar.

## API keys are separate

[API keys](/api-keys/list) authenticate as the organization, not as you, and keep working after you sign out — and after your account is removed from the organization entirely.

If you think your account was compromised, [review the organization's API keys](/api-keys/list) and revoke anything unfamiliar or unused.
