title: "Delete your account"
description: "What deleting your account removes, and what it doesn't."
---

Deleting your Surnex account is separate from deleting an organization, and the distinction matters — one removes your access, the other removes the data.

## Account vs. organization

| Action | Removes | Leaves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Delete an organization](/organizations/settings) | All its projects, data, members, and reports | Your account and other organizations |
| Delete your account | Your login and profile, and organizations only you own | Organizations with another owner |

**An organization you're the only owner of is deleted with your account** — every project and all its data — and its subscription is cancelled. The confirmation names those organizations before you go. An organization with another owner is left as it is; you're simply removed from it.

## How

Go to **Settings → My Account** and click **Delete Account** in the **Danger Zone**. A panel opens listing exactly what goes; type your email address and click **Delete account**. You're signed out straight away.

## Before you delete

**Your own organizations go with you.** Owner can't be passed on, so an organization you created is deleted with your account. If the people in it still need its data, [export](/reports/share) what they need first.

**Export anything you need.** Ranking and backlink history is accumulated day by day and cannot be rebuilt. [Export a PDF report](/reports/share) of anything you want to keep.

**Revoke API keys.** [Keys](/api-keys/list) belong to the organization, not to you, and keep working after your account is gone. If you created keys for integrations that should stop, revoke them yourself.

## What survives

- Organizations with another owner, and their data
- Projects, keywords, and history in those organizations
- [API keys](/api-keys/list) you created in them — organization-scoped, still live
- Reports and any [share links](/reports/share) in them
- Their subscriptions and billing

## Leaving an organization instead

If you only want to stop working with a team, you don't need to delete your account. Ask an admin to remove you on [Users](/organizations/team) — you lose access to that organization and keep your account and everything else.

Note that you can't remove yourself; another admin or the owner has to do it.
